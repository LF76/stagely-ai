Answers & Documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our technology, brand fidelity, and commercial licensing.

How does Stagely prevent warping my brand logos, packaging text, and labels?

Consumer generative tools pass entire photos through image-to-image diffusion, which frequently distorts fine lines and typography. Stagely uses an RGB Invariant Mask: the original product pixels are held completely immutable in coordinate space. Only the perimeter and environment are processed by the diffusion model, which projects shadows and lighting onto the background surface while guaranteeing 100% brand fidelity.

Why is Stagely's generation speed (< 1.5s) so much faster than other AI platforms?

Most AI services spin up cold container instances or route through congested queue systems, causing 20–40 second wait times. Stagely is built natively on Runware's distributed edge infrastructure with pre-warmed weights for Flux Schnell and specialized ControlNet models. This reduces total round-trip inference latency to roughly 1.34 seconds, giving merchants an interactive, live studio experience.

Why don't the staged products look like they are "floating" or pasted on?

Traditional cutout tools slap a transparent PNG onto a background and add an artificial Gaussian drop-shadow, which looks fake to human eyes. Stagely computes MiDaS depth maps and surface normals to establish the exact contact plane (tabletop, pedestal, floor). The diffusion model then synthesizes authentic ambient occlusion, micro-reflections, and contact shadows corresponding to the light source.

Can we process our entire catalog of 500+ SKUs automatically?

Yes. In addition to our interactive studio UI, we provide an asynchronous REST API and webhook queue designed for high-volume catalogs. Enterprise customers can connect their Shopify Plus or BigCommerce store to automatically stage newly uploaded products across 4 standardized brand environments simultaneously.

Who owns the commercial rights and copyright to generated scenes?

You do. You retain 100% full commercial rights, title, and ownership of all output assets. You are free to use them in paid social advertising, print packaging, Amazon A+ content, billboard displays, and physical retail without royalty obligations or platform watermarks.

Are output files high enough resolution for print and billboard campaigns?

Yes. While standard diffusion outputs are limited to 1024×1024 pixels, our pipeline integrates a 4th stage RealESRGAN super-resolution model via Runware, generating ultra-sharp 3840×2160 (4K UHD) files at 300 DPI, suitable for packaging, retail point-of-sale, and high-DPI desktop zoom.
